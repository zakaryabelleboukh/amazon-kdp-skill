# Example: Nonfiction Book Workflow

## Prompt

```text
I want to publish a practical beginner's guide to personal budgeting.

Target reader: young adults who are new to budgeting.
Format: Kindle + paperback.
Tone: practical, friendly and concise.

Create:
1. book positioning
2. chapter outline
3. manuscript plan
4. editing checklist
5. interior specification
6. cover brief
7. KDP metadata draft
8. final QA checklist

Clearly identify assumptions and anything that must be verified before publishing.
```

## Expected workflow

The skill should move from planning to manuscript architecture, editing,
production requirements, metadata and QA without inventing market claims or
Amazon policies.
